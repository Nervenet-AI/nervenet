## One idea. A whole team. All yours.

Nervenet brings tools, files and subagents together to code, research and turn your ideas into work you can review. Less back-and-forth, more finished work.

## Capabilities

| Feature | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Any model** | OpenAI, Anthropic, local servers such as Ollama or LM Studio, and other OpenAI-compatible APIs. Choose the model for each chat. |
| **Works in your folder** | Reads files, edits code, runs commands and researches the web. |
| **Subagents** | Splits big jobs into parts for focused helpers, tracks their work and brings reports back to your main chat. |
| **Restricted mode & approvals** | Asks before risky steps. Approvals stay in your main chat; stop the task to stop its helpers. |
| **Studio** | Opens results in Studio so you can see the work and keep control. |
| **Scheduled chats** | Runs on a schedule to keep work moving around the clock. |
| **Messaging channels** | Message your agent from WhatsApp or Telegram and approve steps from your phone. |
| **Connectors, skills & memory** | Extend what Nervenet can reach, teach it repeatable workflows, and let it remember context. |
| **Your keys stay yours** | Bring your own API keys and choose where your work runs. |

## How it works

```
You ──▶ Nervenet (desktop app) ──▶ your model of choice
              │
              ├── reads & edits files in your project folder
              ├── runs commands and researches the web
              ├── delegates parts of the job to subagents
              └── asks you before risky steps (desktop, WhatsApp or Telegram)
```

## Get started

1. **Download** the latest Windows preview from [Releases](https://github.com/MuhammadNoman76/Nervenet-app/releases).
2. **Connect a model**: add an OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible key, or point it at a local Ollama / LM Studio server.
3. **Pick a folder** for Nervenet to work in.
4. **Give it a job.** Optionally connect WhatsApp or Telegram to stay in the loop from your phone.
